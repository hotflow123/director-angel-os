# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

`Director Angel OS` is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and safe community.
`Director Angel OS` 致力于建设一个尊重他人、重视技术质量、并且安全可靠的社区。

## Our Standard / 我们倡导的行为

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive. 保持尊重、直接、建设性的沟通方式。
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and decisions, not people. 批评应针对想法、代码和决策，而不是针对个人。
- Share reproducible context when reporting bugs, regressions, or security concerns. 报告问题时尽量提供可复现上下文。
- Help keep discussions accessible to contributors across languages and experience levels. 尽量让不同语言背景和经验层次的贡献者都能参与讨论。

## Unacceptable Behavior / 不可接受的行为

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks. 骚扰、歧视、威胁、恐吓或人身攻击。
- Doxxing or exposing private information without permission. 未经许可公开他人隐私或身份信息。
- Bad-faith disruption, spam, or deliberately hostile review behavior. 恶意搅局、刷屏，或带有敌意的评审行为。
- Posting exploit details, credentials, or sensitive operational data in public channels. 在公开渠道发布漏洞利用细节、凭据或敏感运维信息。

## Scope / 适用范围

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release notes, community chats, and project events.
本准则适用于仓库 issue、pull request、discussion、发布说明、社区沟通和项目活动。

## Reporting / 举报与处理

- Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel when possible. 如有条件，请通过私下维护者渠道报告行为问题。
- Do not post personal or sensitive details publicly while escalating a report. 升级问题时不要在公开场合披露个人或敏感信息。
- Maintainers may remove content, pause threads, limit participation, or ban accounts when needed to protect the community. 为保护社区，维护者可以删除内容、暂停讨论、限制参与或封禁账号。
- Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as discreetly as practical. 我们会本着善意审阅举报，并尽可能谨慎处理。

## Project Conduct / 项目协作底线

We expect contributors to raise the technical bar without belittling others.
我们希望贡献者在提高技术标准的同时，不贬低他人。
